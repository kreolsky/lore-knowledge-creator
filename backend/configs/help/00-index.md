# Lore Help

Lore is a collaborative editor for a project's documents: a world, a game, a product, a piece of research. The document is the product; chat, references, notes and the agent exist to produce it.

This guide is an ordinary document with child pages in your project. Every new Lore version overwrites all its pages, including your edits, so keep your own notes elsewhere. A deleted guide is not restored. A guide moved to another project stops updating; every new project gets its own copy.

What the guide does not describe and the "Planned" list does not mention is most likely absent from Lore.

## How to work in Lore

1. **Gather materials** as references of a document: call recordings, voice notes, files, pictures, excerpts. See [References](help:references), [Voice notes](help:voice).
2. **Process them with the agent**: collect what was discussed, flag contradictions and how opinions changed. Package recurring passes as [skills](help:skills); extract facts into [memory](help:memory); automate with [pipelines](help:pipelines).
3. **Pin down the intent.** A document states the goal and the decisions on disputed and borderline cases, and omits what can be unambiguously recovered from context. Minimal is the target.
4. **Fit it into the project**: its place in the tree and its links to the mechanics and decisions it relies on. See [Documents and the graph](help:documents-graph).
5. **Review**: ask the agent for a critical pass against the references and neighbouring documents.

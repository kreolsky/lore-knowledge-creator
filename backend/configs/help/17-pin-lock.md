# Screen and page lock

Both locks use one 4-digit PIN, set in [Profile settings](help:profile), "Quick Lock PIN". The PIN can be changed or removed there.

## Screen lock

Locks the screen without signing out. Press "Lock screen" in the header (it appears once a PIN is set): the editor is covered by the lock screen. To get back, enter the PIN.

## Page lock

Hides one page behind your PIN, for you only. Other members see the page as usual.

1. Open the page, the "Access" tab, "Privacy" section.
2. Tick "Hide this page behind my PIN (only for me)".

Until the PIN is entered:

- the page shows a lock screen instead of its content, and a hover preview of it says it is locked;
- its child pages are hidden in the tree, and a child opened by a link asks for the locked parent's PIN;
- only the Chat, Search and Settings tabs are offered on it;
- chats bound to the page stay out of your chat list;
- the agent acting for you cannot read or find the page, or export it.

After the PIN the page stays open while you move inside its subtree. Once you leave, it locks again after a short grace period (two minutes by default; the administrator can change it or make it lock at once). Locking the screen or signing out locks it at once.

To remove the lock, press "Unlock permanently" on the lock screen, or untick the checkbox, and enter the PIN.
